Why Bigelow

Build something together.

Bigelow is an independent, nonprofit early childhood and preschool program serving children from 3 months to 5 years. Our play-based, emergent curriculum grows from the children's interests, and as a cooperative we benefit from an outstanding team of teachers and the full commitment of our families. Teachers join a supportive community of staff and parents, with ratios that meet or exceed EEC regulations, a light-filled facility with a private outdoor play yard, and real support for professional development.

Assistant Director

We're seeking an Assistant Director to work collaboratively with our Executive Director and manage the daily operations of the center in accordance with Massachusetts licensing regulations. You'll work well as part of a team while being confident working independently, balance multiple priorities, and keep communication clear with staff and families.

What you'll do

  • Manage daily operations, scheduling classroom coverage and keeping policies, records, and EEC licensing requirements up to date
  • Support staff professional development, curriculum planning, and classroom management, including regular classroom observations
  • Coordinate admissions outreach, respond to inquiries, and lead tours for prospective families
  • Work with the Director to support parent committees and maintain a strong cooperative community
  • Cover classrooms as needed to ensure ratios are maintained at all times

What you'll bring

  • EEC Director I certification (required)
  • 3–5 years of experience supervising early childhood staff as a lead teacher or assistant director
  • Familiarity with Massachusetts Department of Early Education and Care regulations
  • BA/BS in Early Childhood Development or a related field (preferred)
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Word and Excel (preferred)
Schedule
Full-time, in person
Compensation
$65,000–$70,000 per year
Benefits
Health, dental, life, and disability insurance; retirement plan; paid time off; parental leave; professional development assistance; free on-site parking
Qualifications
EEC Director I certified

Full-Time Float Teacher

We're seeking a full-time Float Teacher to work 10:15am–5:45pm, Monday through Friday. You'll move between classrooms supporting our teaching teams, and work in our extended day program each afternoon.

What you'll do

  • Float from classroom to classroom covering teacher breaks, planning time, and absences
  • Support teachers in developmentally appropriate practices that meet children's physical, emotional, social, and cognitive needs
  • Meet the individual and group needs of the children in care
  • Keep every child safe in the classroom, on the playground, and in common areas
  • Treat each child with dignity, respect, and appreciation for their cultural background
  • Work in the extended day program from 3:30–5:45pm

What you'll bring

  • EEC certification (required); please include a copy of your certificate with your resume
  • At least 2 years of experience (preferred)
  • Ability to meet all health, safety, and background check requirements of the MA Department of Early Education and Care
Schedule
Full-time, 37.5 hours per week, Monday–Friday, 10:15am–5:45pm
Compensation
$24–$26 per hour, depending on experience
Benefits
11 paid holidays; 5.5 weeks of vacation (two weeks of your choosing, plus winter holidays, April vacation, and 1.5 weeks in August); sick days; health, dental, life, and disability insurance; matching Simple IRA contributions; longevity bonuses after three years; parental leave; professional development assistance
Qualifications
EEC certified; 2+ years of experience preferred
